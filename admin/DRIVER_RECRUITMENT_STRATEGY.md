# Driver Recruitment Strategy - Connecta

## Overview
Strategic plan to recruit 100-500 verified drivers in Nairobi within 3-6 months.

---

## Phase 1: Initial Recruitment (Month 1-2) - Target: 50 Drivers

### Online Channels

1. **Social Media Campaigns**
   - **Facebook Groups**:
     - Boda Boda Riders Kenya
     - Nairobi Taxi Drivers
     - Uber/Bolt Driver Groups
   - **Instagram/TikTok**:
     - Short videos showing earnings potential
     - Driver testimonials
     - Day-in-the-life content
   - **Budget**: KSh 20,000-50,000/month

2. **WhatsApp Marketing**
   - Create driver recruitment WhatsApp group
   - Share in existing driver communities
   - Referral program (driver refers driver)
   - **Cost**: Free

3. **Google Ads**
   - Target keywords: "driver jobs Nairobi", "boda boda jobs"
   - Location: Nairobi and surrounding areas
   - **Budget**: KSh 30,000-50,000/month

### Offline Channels

1. **Boda Boda Stages**
   - Visit popular stages (CBD, Westlands, Eastleigh)
   - Distribute flyers with QR code to onboarding form
   - Face-to-face recruitment
   - **Cost**: KSh 10,000 (printing + transport)

2. **Taxi/Matatu Saccos**
   - Partner with existing saccos
   - Offer group onboarding sessions
   - Bulk registration discounts
   - **Cost**: Free (partnership)

3. **Posters & Banners**
   - Strategic locations: petrol stations, garages, busy junctions
   - Include QR code for easy signup
   - **Cost**: KSh 15,000-30,000

### Incentives (Month 1-2)

1. **Sign-up Bonus**: KSh 1,000 after first 10 rides
2. **Zero Commission**: First week commission-free
3. **Referral Bonus**: KSh 500 for each driver referred
4. **Top Earner Rewards**: Weekly prizes for top 10 drivers

**Total Phase 1 Budget**: KSh 75,000-150,000

## Driver Verification Process

### Step 1: Online Application (Automated)
- Driver fills onboarding form
- Uploads required documents
- System validates basic info
- **Time**: 10-15 minutes

### Step 2: Document Verification (Manual)
- Admin reviews documents
- Checks license validity
- Verifies insurance
- Background check (optional)
- **Time**: 24-48 hours

### Step 3: Interview (Optional)
- Phone or in-person interview
- Assess communication skills
- Explain platform rules
- **Time**: 15-30 minutes

### Step 4: Training
- Platform tutorial video
- Safety guidelines
- Customer service tips
- **Time**: 1-2 hours (online)

### Step 5: Activation
- Account activated
- Driver app access granted
- Welcome bonus credited
- **Time**: Immediate

## Driver Retention Strategy

### Financial Incentives
1. **Weekly Payouts**: Fast payment processing
2. **Loyalty Bonuses**: Long-term driver rewards
3. **Surge Pricing**: Higher earnings during peak hours
4. **Tips**: 100% to driver

### Support & Benefits
1. **24/7 Support**: Dedicated driver support line
2. **Insurance**: Accident coverage while driving
3. **Fuel Discounts**: Partner with fuel stations
4. **Maintenance**: Discounted vehicle services

### Community Building
1. **Driver WhatsApp Groups**: City-specific groups
2. **Monthly Meetups**: Networking events
3. **Recognition**: Driver of the month awards
4. **Feedback**: Regular surveys and improvements

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Recruitment Metrics
- Applications per week: Target 50-100
- Approval rate: Target 70-80%
- Time to activation: Target <48 hours
- Cost per driver: Target <KSh 3,000

### Retention Metrics
- Active drivers (weekly): Target 80%
- Driver churn rate: Target <10%/month
- Average rides per driver: Target 20-30/week
- Driver satisfaction: Target 4.5+/5

### Quality Metrics
- Driver rating: Target 4.7+/5
- Acceptance rate: Target 85%+
- Cancellation rate: Target <5%
- Response time: Target <2 minutes
